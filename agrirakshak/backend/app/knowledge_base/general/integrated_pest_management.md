# Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Principles

**Crop**: General Agriculture  
**Topic**: Integrated Pest Management  

## Principles
- **Host Resistance**: Grow certified disease-resistant crop varieties suited for your region.
- **Cultural Control**: Practice crop rotation, field sanitation, intercropping, and proper spacing.
- **Physical/Mechanical Control**: Use yellow sticky traps, light traps, bird perches, and manual weed/pest collection.
- **Biological Control**: Preserve natural predators (ladybird beetles, spiders, parasitoids) and use bio-pesticides (*Neem*, *Trichoderma*, *Bt*).
- **Chemical Control**: Use chemical pesticides strictly as a last resort at Economic Threshold Levels (ETL).

## Recommended Guidelines
- Regularly monitor crops twice a week.
- Keep field bunds clean of alternate weed hosts.
- Never mix incompatible chemical pesticides.

## Safety Precautions
- Use protective gear (gloves, mask, apron) during handling.
