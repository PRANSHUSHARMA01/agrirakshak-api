# Fertilizer Application & Soil Health Safety

**Crop**: General  
**Topic**: Fertilizer Safety  

## Guidelines
- Apply fertilizers based strictly on Soil Health Card recommendations.
- Apply Nitrogen in split doses (basal, tillering, and panicle initiation) to reduce leaf lushness that attracts sap-sucking pests and fungi.
- Combine chemical fertilizers with organic Farm Yard Manure (FYM) or compost.
- Apply Potash to enhance natural crop immunity against leaf diseases.
