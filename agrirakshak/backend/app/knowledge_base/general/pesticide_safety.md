# Pesticide Handling and Application Safety Precautions

**Crop**: All Crops  
**Topic**: Safe Pesticide Application Rules  

## Safety Instructions
- Always read pesticide container label carefully before mixing.
- Use recommended dosage; do not under-dose or over-dose.
- Wear mask, gloves, goggles, and protective clothing during spraying.
- Spray in the direction of wind, never against the wind.
- Do not eat, drink, or smoke while spraying pesticides.
- Wash hands, face, and clothes thoroughly with soap after spraying.
- Dispose of empty chemical containers by puncturing and burying away from water sources.

## Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI)
- Observe the mandatory Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) stated on product label before harvesting crops for human consumption.
