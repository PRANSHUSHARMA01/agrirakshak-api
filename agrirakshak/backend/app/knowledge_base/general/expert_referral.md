# Agricultural Extension & Expert Referral Guidelines

**Crop**: General  
**Topic**: When to Seek Officer / KVK Assistance  

## Contact Local Experts When:
- Symptoms cannot be identified with high confidence.
- Symptoms spread rapidly across >15% of the field within 48 hours.
- Chemical/cultural treatments fail to control the issue.
- Unusual pest or unknown wilt symptoms appear.
