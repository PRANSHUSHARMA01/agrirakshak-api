# Irrigation Management and Fungal Risk Guidelines

**Crop**: All Crops  
**Topic**: Irrigation Best Practices  

## Key Guidelines
- Avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation late in the evening as wet leaves overnight facilitate fungal spore germination (Blast, Rust, Blight).
- Practice Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) in rice fields to save water and reduce root rot/bacterial disease development.
- Ensure proper surface field drainage during heavy rain spells.
