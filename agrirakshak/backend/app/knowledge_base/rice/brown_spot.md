# Brown Spot of Rice (Bipolaris oryzae)

**Crop**: Rice  
**Disease Name**: Brown Spot / Helminthosporium Leaf Spot  

## Symptoms
- Oval brown spots with yellow halo on leaves and glumes.
- Spots coalesce leading to leaf drying.
- Infected seeds become discolored and fail to germinate.

## Favorable Conditions
- Nutrient-deficient soils (poor Potash and Zinc availability).
- Drought stress or irregular irrigation.
- Temperature 25°C to 30°C with high humidity.

## Prevention
- Soil test driven fertilizer application; ensure adequate Potash and Micronutrients (Zinc Sulfate 25 kg/ha).
- Use disease-free seeds.

## Cultural Control
- Maintain optimal soil moisture.
- Incorporate organic compost / FYM @ 10 tonnes/ha.

## Biological Control
- Seed treatment with *Trichoderma viride* @ 4g/kg seed.

## Chemical Control
- Seed treatment with Thiram @ 3g/kg seed.
- Foliar spray of Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2g/liter or Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 ml/liter.

## Safety Precautions
- Avoid contact with eyes and skin while mixing fungicides.

## When to Contact Expert
- If spots cover more than 30% leaf surface before flowering.
