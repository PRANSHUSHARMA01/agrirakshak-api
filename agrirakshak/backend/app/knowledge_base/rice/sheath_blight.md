# Sheath Blight of Rice (Rhizoctonia solani)

**Crop**: Rice  
**Disease Name**: Sheath Blight  

## Symptoms
- Oval or irregular greenish-gray lesions on leaf sheaths near water line.
- Lesions enlarge with dark brown borders resembling snake skin patterns.
- Sclerotia (mustard seed like balls) form on infected sheaths.

## Favorable Conditions
- High relative humidity (85-100%).
- Temperature 28°C - 32°C.
- Dense plant population and heavy nitrogenous manure.

## Prevention
- Maintain wide spacing (20cm x 15cm).
- Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilization.

## Cultural Control
- Destroy infected stubbles and weeds along irrigation channels.

## Biological Control
- Soil application of *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 2.5 kg/ha mixed with 50 kg FYM.

## Chemical Control
- Spray Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 2 ml/liter OR Validamycin 3% L @ 2 ml/liter targeted at lower stem sheaths.

## Safety Precautions
- Direct spray wand towards leaf sheaths near soil surface.

## When to Contact Expert
- If lesions reach upper flag leaves before panicle emergence.

## Sources
- Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage (DPPQS)
