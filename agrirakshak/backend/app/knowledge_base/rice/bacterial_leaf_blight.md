# Bacterial Leaf Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae)

**Crop**: Rice  
**Disease Name**: Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB) / Kresek Phase  

## Symptoms
- Water-soaked to yellow-orange stripes along leaf margins.
- Wavy margins starting from leaf tips moving downwards.
- Leaves dry up and turn straw-colored ("kresek" wilt phase in seedlings).
- Milky bacterial ooze droplets visible on young leaves in early morning.

## Favorable Conditions
- High temperatures (25°C - 34°C) with heavy rainfall and strong winds.
- Deep stagnant water in fields.
- High nitrogen levels.

## Prevention
- Grow resistant varieties (e.g., Improved Samba Mahsuri, PR 126).
- Ensure field drainage; avoid continuous flooding.
- Balance fertilizers: ratio N:P:K = 120:60:60 kg/ha.

## Cultural Control
- Clip top leaf tips of seedlings before transplanting carefully to avoid root damage.
- Destroy weed hosts along bunds.

## Biological Control
- Spray *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 5g/liter at 15-day intervals.

## Chemical Control
- Spray Copper Hydroxide 77% WP @ 2g/liter OR Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 3g/liter mixed with Streptocycline @ 0.1g/liter.

## Safety Precautions
- Do not spray against the wind direction.
- Keep children and livestock away during chemical application.

## When to Contact Expert
- When kresek seedling wilt occurs across >10% of field.
