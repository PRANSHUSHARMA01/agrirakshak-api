# Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae)

**Crop**: Rice  
**Disease Name**: Rice Blast / Pyricularia oryzae  

## Symptoms
- Spindle-shaped spots on leaves with gray or white centers and brown/reddish margins.
- Lesions on leaf sheaths, neck, and panicles.
- Severe neck rot causes panicles to fall over ("broken neck").

## Favorable Conditions
- High relative humidity (>90%).
- Prolonged leaf wetness from dew or fog.
- Temperatures between 20°C and 28°C.
- Excessive nitrogen fertilizer application.

## Prevention
- Use resistant certified seed varieties (e.g., Swarna, Pusa Basmati 1).
- Avoid excessive nitrogen doses; split nitrogen applications into 3 doses.
- Maintain proper plant spacing to allow leaf drying.

## Cultural Control
- Remove crop residues and burn or plow stubble immediately after harvest.
- Keep field submerged during early vegetative stage to suppress spore development.

## Biological Control
- Seed treatment with *Pseudomonas fluorescens* (10g/kg of seed).
- Foliar application of *Trichoderma harzianum*.

## Chemical Control
- Seed treatment with Carbendazim 50% WP @ 2g/kg seed.
- Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6g/liter of water at initial symptom appearance.
- Alternate with Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 1.5 ml/liter if neck blast threatens.

## Safety Precautions
- Wear protective mask, gloves, and long sleeves during chemical spraying.
- Observe 14-day pre-harvest interval (PHI).

## When to Contact Expert
- If panicle blast exceeds 5% of the field area.
- If symptoms spread despite recommended fungicide spray.
