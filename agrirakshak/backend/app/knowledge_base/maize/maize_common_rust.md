# Maize Common Rust (Puccinia sorghi)

**Crop**: Maize / Corn  
**Disease Name**: Common Rust  

## Symptoms
- Golden-brown to cinnamon-brown pustules on both upper and lower leaf surfaces.
- Pustules rupture late in the season turning black.

## Favorable Conditions
- Cool temperatures (16°C to 23°C).
- High humidity (>100% dew formation).

## Prevention
- Plant resistant hybrids (e.g., HQPM-1, Bio 9681).

## Cultural Control
- Early planting to avoid cool humid peak spore dispersal periods.

## Biological Control
- Spray bio-fungicide *Trichoderma viride* @ 5g/liter.

## Chemical Control
- Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2g/liter OR Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 ml/liter if rust covers >5% leaf area.

## Safety Precautions
- Wear safety goggles and gloves when preparing spray solution.

## When to Contact Expert
- If rust appears before silking stage.
