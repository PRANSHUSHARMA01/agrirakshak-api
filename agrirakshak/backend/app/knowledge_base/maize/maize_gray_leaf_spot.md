# Maize Gray Leaf Spot (Cercospora zeae-maydis)

**Crop**: Maize  
**Disease Name**: Gray Leaf Spot (GLS)  

## Symptoms
- Rectangular tan to gray lesions strictly delimited by leaf veins.
- Severe blighting leads to premature leaf death and stalk lodging.

## Favorable Conditions
- High humidity and extended leaf wetness.
- Temperatures 22°C to 30°C.

## Prevention
- Minimum 2-year crop rotation out of maize.
- Plant resistant hybrids.

## Cultural Control
- Clean cultivation and destruction of infected corn debris.

## Biological Control
- Foliar spray of bio-fungicide formulations.

## Chemical Control
- Spray Pyraclostrobin 20% WG @ 1g/liter or Azoxystrobin + Difenoconazole combination.

## Safety Precautions
- Use personal protective equipment (PPE).

## When to Contact Expert
- If blighting spreads to ear leaves prior to grain fill.

## Sources
- International Maize and Wheat Improvement Center (CIMMYT)
