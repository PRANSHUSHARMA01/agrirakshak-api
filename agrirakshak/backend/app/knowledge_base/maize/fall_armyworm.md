# Fall Armyworm in Maize (Spodoptera frugiperda)

**Crop**: Maize  
**Pest Name**: Fall Armyworm (FAW)  

## Symptoms
- Pin-hole damage on young whorl leaves.
- Ragged, irregular holes on expanding leaves.
- Heavy moist frass (excreta) accumulation inside leaf whorls.
- Caterpillars feature an inverted 'Y' mark on the head and 4 square spots on the tail end.

## Favorable Conditions
- Dry warm spells following early rains.
- Continuous monoculture maize planting.

## Prevention
- Deep plowing to expose pupae to birds and sunlight.
- Synchronized planting across adjacent farms.

## Cultural Control
- Handpick egg masses and young larvae in small fields.
- Apply clean sand, soil, or sawdust mixed with neem cake (9:1) into leaf whorls.

## Biological Control
- Release *Trichogramma pretiosum* egg parasitoids @ 50,000/acre.
- Apply *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Bt) formulations @ 2g/liter or Metarhizium anisopliae @ 5g/liter.

## Chemical Control
- Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4g/liter targeted directly into leaf whorls.
- Alternate with Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.5 ml/liter or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.4 ml/liter.

## Safety Precautions
- Direct nozzle into the central whorl cone.
- Observe mandatory safety pre-harvest interval of 14 days.

## When to Contact Expert
- When whorl damage exceeds 10% of plants at 15-30 days post-emergence.
