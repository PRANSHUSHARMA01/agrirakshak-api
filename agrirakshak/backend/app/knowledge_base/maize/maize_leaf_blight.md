# Turcicum Leaf Blight / Northern Corn Leaf Blight (Exserohilum turcicum)

**Crop**: Maize  
**Disease Name**: Turcicum Leaf Blight (TLB)  

## Symptoms
- Long, elliptical, grayish-green or tan lesions on leaves (2.5 cm to 15 cm long).
- Dark grayish fungal spore masses visible inside lesions during wet weather.

## Favorable Conditions
- Moderate temperatures (18°C to 27°C).
- Heavy dew and cloudy rainy weather.

## Prevention
- Crop rotation with non-host crops like pulses or oilseeds.
- Use certified blight-tolerant maize seeds.

## Cultural Control
- Deep plowing of crop residues post-harvest.

## Biological Control
- Seed treatment with *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10g/kg seed.

## Chemical Control
- Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5g/liter OR Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 ml/liter at 10-14 day intervals.

## Safety Precautions
- Do not enter sprayed field for 24 hours.

## When to Contact Expert
- When lesions spread above ear height before pollination.
