# Integrated Pest and Disease Management (IPDM) Guide - Potato Crops

## Potato___Early_blight (Alternaria solani)
### Symptoms
Concentric ring target-board dark brown spots on older lower leaves, progressing upwards. Leaves yellow and dry out.

### Cultural Control
- Maintain optimum plant vigor through adequate Nitrogen and Potassium.
- Avoid sprinkler irrigation late in the evening.

### Chemical Control & Safe Dosage
- **Mancozeb 75% WP**: 2g per liter of water.
- **Chlorothalonil 75% WP**: 2g per liter of water.
- **PHI**: 14 days.

## Potato___Late_blight (Phytophthora infestans)
### Symptoms
Rapidly expanding water-soaked dark brown-to-black lesions with white cottony fungal growth on leaf undersides in high humidity (>80%). Destroys canopy within days.

### Cultural Control
- Earthing up soil around tubers to prevent zoospore wash into tubers.
- Destroy volunteer plants and infected crop debris.

### Chemical Control & Safe Dosage
- **Preventive**: Mancozeb 75% WP at 2.5g/L or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP at 3g/L.
- **Curative / Systemic**: Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP at 2.5g/L or Dimethomorph 50% WP at 1g/L.
- **PHI**: 14-21 days.
