# Integrated Pest and Disease Management (IPDM) Guide - Maize Crops

## 02_fall_armyworm & 03_FAW_symptoms (Spodoptera frugiperda)
### Symptoms
Ragged whorl feeding holes, heavy sawdust-like frass inside the central funnel, and bored cobs. Larvae have a distinctive inverted 'Y' mark on the head and 4 black spots in a square on the 8th abdominal segment.

### Cultural Control
- Deep plowing in summer to expose pupae to birds and sunlight.
- Intercropping with Desmodium or cowpea (Push-Pull strategy).
- Hand-picking egg masses and applying clean sand or ash into whorls.

### Biological Control
- Spray *Metarhizium anisopliae* or *Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt)* at 2g/L water during early larval stages.
- Install pheromone traps at 10-15 traps/ha.

### Chemical Control & Safe Dosage
- **Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG**: 0.4g per liter of water (200g/ha) directed into the central whorl.
- **Spinetoram 11.7% SC**: 0.5 ml per liter of water (250 ml/ha).
- **PHI**: 14 days. Rotate chemical classes to prevent resistance.

## 01_maydis_leaf_blight (Bipolaris maydis) & 02_turcicum_leaf_blight (Exserohilum turcicum)
### Symptoms
Maydis Blight: Small rectangular tan lesions bounded by leaf veins.
Turcicum Blight: Large long spindle-shaped elliptical grayish-green or tan lesions (5-15 cm long).

### Cultural Control
- Use resistant hybrids (e.g. HQPM 1, DHM 117).
- Destroy infected crop residue post-harvest.

### Chemical Control & Safe Dosage
- **Mancozeb 75% WP**: 2.5g per liter of water.
- **Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC**: 1 ml per liter of water.
- **PHI**: 21 days.

## 01_aphid (Rhopalosiphum maidis)
### Symptoms
Colonies of dark green soft-bodied insects sucking sap on young leaves and tassels, secreting sticky honeydew followed by black sooty mold.

### Control
- **Neem Oil 10,000 ppm**: 3 ml per liter of water with soap solution.
- **Imidacloprid 17.8% SL**: 0.3 ml per liter of water if infestation exceeds threshold.
- **PHI**: 15 days.
