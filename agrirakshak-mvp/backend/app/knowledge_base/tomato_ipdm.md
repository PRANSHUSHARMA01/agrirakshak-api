# Integrated Pest and Disease Management (IPDM) Guide - Tomato Crops

## Tomato_Bacterial_spot (Xanthomonas perforans)
### Symptoms
Small dark water-soaked spots on leaves with yellow halos; spots dry out and drop out leaving shot-holes. Fruit develops raised scab-like spots.

### Control
- **Copper Hydroxide 77% WP**: 2g/L + **Streptocycline**: 0.1g/L.
- **PHI**: 7 days.

---

## Tomato_Early_blight & Tomato_Late_blight
### Early Blight Symptoms
Concentric rings on leaves and dark leathery sunken lesions at the stem end of fruits.
### Late Blight Symptoms
Water-soaked lesions on foliage, white mold growth on undersides, and firm brown greasy fruit rot.

### Control
- **Azoxystrobin 23% SC**: 1 ml/L of water.
- **Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP**: 2.5g/L of water.
- **PHI**: 7 days for Azoxystrobin, 14 days for Metalaxyl+Mancozeb.

---

## Tomato__Tomato_YellowLeaf__Curl_Virus (TYLCV) & Tomato_Spider_mites
### Symptoms
TYLCV: Severe stunting, upward curling of leaf margins, yellowing, and bushy appearance caused by Whitefly vector (*Bemisia tabaci*).
Spider Mites: Fine webbing, speckling/stippling on leaf surface turning bronze.

### Vector & Pest Control
- Install Yellow Sticky Traps (20/ha) for Whitefly monitoring.
- **Imidacloprid 17.8% SL**: 0.5 ml/L for whitefly control.
- **Abamectin 1.9% EC**: 0.5 ml/L for spider mites.
- **PHI**: 7 days.
