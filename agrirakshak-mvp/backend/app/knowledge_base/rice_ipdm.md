# Integrated Pest and Disease Management (IPDM) Guide - Rice Crops

## 01_Bacterial_leaf_blight (Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae)
### Symptoms
Water-soaked lesions on leaf margins that rapidly turn yellow and dry into bleached straw-colored stripes with wavy margins. Bacterial ooze drops may appear on young leaves in humid mornings.

### Cultural Control
- Use balanced nitrogen fertilization; avoid excessive urea applications during humid weather.
- Ensure proper field drainage and avoid flooding infected fields.
- Plant resistant rice cultivars such as Swarna Sub1 or PR 126.

### Biological Control
- Apply *Pseudomonas fluorescens* seed treatment at 10g/kg seed.
- Spray *Bacillus subtilis* formulation at 5g/liter of water during early symptom onset.

### Chemical Control & Safe Dosage
- **Copper Oxychloride 50% WP**: 2.5g per liter of water (1000g/ha in 500L water).
- **Streptocycline (Streptomycin sulphate + Tetracycline)**: 6g per 60 liters of water (100mg/L).
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI)**: Maintain 15 days PHI before harvesting. Always wear protective masks and gloves when spraying.

## 02_Brown_spot (Bipolaris oryzae)
### Symptoms
Small circular to oval sesame-seed shaped brown spots with yellow halos across leaf surfaces. Severe infection leads to grain discoloration and seedling blight.

### Cultural Control
- Correct soil nutrient deficiencies, especially Potassium and Manganese.
- Use certified pathogen-free seeds and practice crop rotation with legumes.

### Biological Control
- Seed treatment with *Trichoderma viride* at 4g/kg seed.

### Chemical Control & Safe Dosage
- **Mancozeb 75% WP**: 2g per liter of water (1 kg/ha).
- **Propiconazole 25% EC**: 1ml per liter of water (500 ml/ha).
- **PHI**: 21 days for Propiconazole.

## 03_False_smut (Ustilaginoidea virens)
### Symptoms
Individual grains in the panicle transform into large, velvety orange-yellow balls that later turn greenish-black.

### Cultural Control
- Avoid excess nitrogen application at flowering stage.
- Remove and destroy infected panicles manually before smut spores disperse.

### Chemical Control & Safe Dosage
- **Copper Hydroxide 77% WP**: 2g/L water at boot-leaf stage.
- **Tebuconazole 50% + Trifloxystrobin 25% WG**: 0.4g/L water (200g/ha).
- **PHI**: 30 days. Spray strictly before panicle emergence.

## 04_leaf_sheath_blight (Rhizoctonia solani)
### Symptoms
Oval or greenish-grey lesions with brown borders on leaf sheaths near the waterline. Lesions enlarge and coalesce, causing lodging.

### Cultural Control
- Reduce planting density to improve canopy airflow.
- Drain field periodically to desiccate sclerotia.

### Chemical Control & Safe Dosage
- **Validamycin 3% L**: 2 ml per liter of water (1000 ml/ha).
- **Hexaconazole 5% EC**: 2 ml per liter of water.
- **PHI**: 14 days for Validamycin.

## 07_White_stem_borer & 08_Yellow_stem_borer (Scirpophaga spp.)
### Symptoms
Causes 'Dead Heart' in vegetative stages (central tillers dry out and pull easily) and 'White Head' in reproductive stages (empty, bleached panicles).

### Cultural Control
- Clip leaf tips before transplanting to destroy egg masses.
- Harvest close to ground level and plow stubble promptly.

### Biological Control
- Release *Trichogramma japonicum* egg parasitoids at 100,000/ha weekly.
- Install Pheromone traps at 20 traps/ha for monitoring.

### Chemical Control & Safe Dosage
- **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC**: 0.3 ml per liter of water (150 ml/ha).
- **Cartap Hydrochloride 4G**: 25 kg/ha applied as granules in shallow water.
- **PHI**: 21 days.
